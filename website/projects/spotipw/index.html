Release notes from the author

0.22.0 (2026-09-23)

Features

  • Apple Music's animated album cover on the lock screen where the track has no Canvas (e0d5538
  • audio effects run on the mod's own engine, libjamesdsp is gone (413b2d3
  • find in playlist as a glass search bar above the header, shown on pull-down (#81 (36ed0a7
  • line meanings from Genius on the lyrics, a bubble for the artist's own and an underline for the rest, opened in a sheet (950baf7
  • Mod > Licenses shows the mod's license and the full text of the third-party ones (c790445
  • play the track's Canvas as the lock screen's animated artwork (c4b75e3
  • the artist's Follow is a glyph that turns into a checkmark, as add to library does (#88 (f10bf8d

Fixes

  • a long note under a settings section shows in full instead of ending in an ellipsis (a6b1876
  • add to library turns into a checkmark, and Follow no longer flips from a glyph to a word (#86, #88 (e524034
  • the player's more menu no longer hangs on loading and greys out lyrics for tracks no source has lyrics for (#93 (283ac30
  • the playlist's cover keeps its size and its fade after the page is pulled down past the top (a83562c
  • the redesigned player can no longer be scrolled up (#83 (62eee33

What it is

Spotify, in glass.

A no-jailbreak Theos tweak that rebuilds Spotify for iOS around a Liquid Glass design: a glass tab bar, player and lyrics, a Mod Settings panel, Live Activity, accent colours, gestures, blocked artists, and speed and pitch control.

Liquid Glass redesign of the Spotify iOS interface

Inside a build

How this component is used

What ships

A compatible build of the tweak is installed into the app as Frameworks/spotifyglass.dylib, with its MobileSubstrate filter and the CydiaSubstrate.framework that the proven upstream build ships alongside it.

The exact version integrated in each build is recorded in that build's manifest and shown on the download.

Building it from source

Building the upstream tweak from source works exactly as upstream documents it, with Theos on macOS. PwEevee's own pipeline does not replace or fork that process — it consumes a compatible build of the result.

See docs/tweaks.md in the repository and the upstream repository itself.
